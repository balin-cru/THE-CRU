Scene 03 — About

The Cru

Twelve years behind the camera across features, documentaries and branded work, with an eye trained on how shadow tells half the story. We shoot like the light is running out — because on set, it usually is.

Our Focus

Three things, done properly.

We built The Cru around the work we actually want to be doing: stories that need patience, places that don't make it easy, and ideas that need a design eye as much as a camera operator. That's documentary, outdoor and adventure work, and the creative thinking that sits behind both.
